# Bayside Roof Co. — Copper Edition

Second standalone homepage concept for Brighton, Melbourne. New charcoal, copper-orange and white design, with full-width photography, editorial content, an interactive service showcase and a larger image collection.

## Open

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build tools, CDN, installation or internet connection are needed for the downloaded page. Keep the CSS, JavaScript and assets alongside the HTML file.

## Included

- `index.html` — complete responsive homepage.
- `style.css` — core layout, branding and visual theme.
- `responsive.css` — tablet and mobile layouts.
- `animations.css` — restrained motion with reduced-motion support.
- `script.js` — mobile menu, service accordions, image gallery and enquiry preview.
- `assets/` — thirteen website photographs in optimised WebP format, plus self-hosted fonts.
- `source-images/` — all thirteen original-resolution generated PNG images. These account for the larger downloadable package and are kept separate from the assets loaded by the website.
- `IMAGE-PROMPTS.md` — exact image-generation briefs and provenance notes.

## Interaction

The service list changes its supporting image and description. Quick links below the hero select the relevant service. Service enquiry links preselect the form option. Gallery images open in a keyboard-accessible native dialog with previous/next controls. FAQ rows use native disclosures. All form output is plain text and personal information is never sent or persisted.

## Business content

Bayside Roof Co. is a proposed brand. The site is a design concept, with generated illustrative imagery rather than verified company staff or completed projects. There are no fabricated customer testimonials, star ratings, licence numbers, guarantees, awards or business-history claims.

Confirm the real business name, service coverage, contact details and service wording before business launch. The labelled enquiry form validates fields and previews an enquiry only; it needs an actual submission service and appropriate privacy information to collect live enquiries.

The first blue-and-white homepage is separate and is not overwritten by this design.
